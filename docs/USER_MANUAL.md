# NOVA X - User Manual

Welcome to NOVA X, your AI Co-Founder. Follow this guide to turn your idea into a pitch-ready startup.

## 1. Getting Started
*   Navigate to the homepage and click **Sign Up**. 
*   If you are evaluating the platform, you can use the **Demo Mode** by clicking the "Demo Mode" link on the login page (or navigating to `/login?demo=true`).

## 2. Creating a Project
*   Once logged in, you will see your Dashboard.
*   Click **New Project**.
*   Enter a basic name and description for your idea. Don't worry about perfection; the AI will help you refine it.

## 3. The AI Generation Workflow
Navigate through the tabs on the left sidebar sequentially for the best results:
1.  **Problem Discovery**: Define the pain points. Click "Generate" to have the AI perform a root-cause analysis.
2.  **Innovation DNA**: Based on your problem, let the AI generate your Unique Value Proposition (UVP).
3.  **Startup Formation**: Generate your startup's name, tagline, and brand personality.
4.  **Market Intelligence**: The AI will generate a realistic TAM/SAM/SOM breakdown and SWOT analysis.
5.  **Financial Planning**: Enter your initial constraints and let the AI estimate your required funding and runway.

## 4. AI Co-Founder (Chat)
*   At any time, navigate to the **Co-Founder** tab.
*   Ask questions like *"How should I price this product?"* The AI will answer utilizing the context of everything generated in Step 3.

## 5. Exports
*   Navigate to the **Investor Hub**.
*   Click **Export to PDF** to generate an Executive Summary.
*   Click **Export Pitch Deck** to receive a structured JSON ready to be imported into presentation software.
